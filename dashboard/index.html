How this works and its limits
  • Greedy set-cover heuristic; not guaranteed minimum.
  • Candidate locations are settlement-centre proxies.
  • SRTM terrain + LoS + 700 MHz 60%-Fresnel screen.
  • No land, access, clutter, vegetation, interference or capacity validation.
  • Power is screened by nighttime light only. Dark means no radiance VIIRS can detect, never a surveyed grid connection.
  • Preliminary screening, not predicted coverage or engineering design.
  • Blank map areas do not mean no coverage.
